Paw 0.7.1 — desktop pet polish and growing-up calendar

- Adjustable allowed actions: hold a single pose, enable all, and independent previews. Feeding and dragging remain available.
- Paused pets no longer walk in place or walk toward a toy/home. Personality changes affect activity choices, rest, alertness and movement pace, with a live slider preview.
- Feeding bowls stay on the floor. A muzzle/rim clearance constraint prevents the face intersecting the bowl while leaning down.
- Separate Home & habitat section with persistent size, structure color, fabric/accent color, visibility and construction controls. Existing construction progress is retained.
- Reorganized pet, personality, habitat, wardrobe, activity and application settings; compact right-click menu with a direct Settings destination.
- Shallow corneas, orbital rims, continuous tapered limbs, rounded toes and surface-fitted markings. All nine original animals, breeds and outfits remain available.
- Longer staged departure/arrival transitions coalesce rapid changes and dispose old models; reduced motion skips travel effects.
- Removed the unavailable computer temperature reading and hiding animation. City weather remains available.
- Calendar starts at day one, retains the original birth date, includes offline days and DST, and drives gradual size/proportion changes and growth stages. Existing pet age/progress is migrated without resetting currency, inventory or friendship.

Validation: deterministic activity/persistence/calendar tests; browser UI and real WebGL checks; Linux Electron native-window checks for feeding, held pose, native roaming, house resizing/moving and progress migration. Windows and macOS installers are built on their target runners. Real consumer Windows/macOS hardware, battery consumption and GPU-specific behavior have not been manually tested.

Install: Windows x64 Setup.exe; macOS Apple Silicon arm64 or Intel x64 ZIP (extract Paw.app into Applications). The separate ASAR includes the application source and metadata for subsequent work. Builds are unsigned; no signing identity was supplied.
